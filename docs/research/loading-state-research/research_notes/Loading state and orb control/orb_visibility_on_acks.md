## KQ1. Full timeline of one turn that produces an Ack and later a Result (and who shows/hides the orb)

### Takeaway
For a mic turn the Rust side emits `State(Thinking)`, `Ack`, `Loading(true)` (+ `stage:loading_rect`/`stage:loading_visible`) back-to-back in about 2 ms, then later `Loading(false)`, `Result`, and clears the request. The frontend never hides the orb in response to the Ack itself; the orb is shown by whatever called `speak()` and hidden only by (a) the "idle means hidden" watchdog, (b) `hideOrbAfterSpeech` chains, or (c) explicit `setVisible(false)` calls elsewhere. In the two real ack turns in the log the orb stayed on screen the whole time.

## KQ2. Which code calls `hideOrbAfterSpeech(...)` / `setVisible(false)` in the ack path, and what is the intended design versus what happens

### Takeaway
The intended design (comments at orch.ts 541-544, 563-564, 579-580 and rec 922-925) is "ack plays -> orb disappears -> loading indicator carries the turn". The implementation after the "never hide while speaking" rewrite only hides when `state === "idle"`, and nothing sets `idle` after an ack, so on the Worker/MCP/GitHub paths the hide chains are mostly no-ops and the orb is left in a silent "speaking" (or "thinking") pose beside the spinner. The hide really does happen on paths where the ack is delivered as a `Result` (screen tour, CodeRabbit, "Give me a minute, sir.").

### Cited Findings
- **Exact `hideOrbAfterSpeech` logic.** At fire time: (1) `ghostActive` -> reset counter, do nothing; (2) `state === "speaking"` -> if `isRustTtsPlaying()` re-arm in 1000 ms, else increment module-global `stuckSpeakingTicks` and re-arm; at 10 ticks (`STUCK_SPEAKING_LIMIT`) log "stuck-speaking watchdog: silent 10s" and call `finishSpokenResult(getCurrentRequestId() ?? "")`, ending the chain; (3) `state !== "idle"` (thinking, listening) -> **return, chain ends, no re-arm**; (4) idle -> if `captionActive` re-arm in 350 ms up to 43 times (~15 s), else `setVisible(false)`. [CODE] — [orch.ts 431-494](D:\ULTRON\frontend\src\net\orchestrator.ts)
- **Callers in the ack path.** `loading(true)` -> `hideOrbAfterSpeech(600)` (orch.ts:545); `ack` with local ack already given -> `hideOrbAfterSpeech(1500)` (:565); `ack` deferred callback -> `hideOrbAfterSpeech(1500)` (:581). Other callers: `OrbFrame` on ghost session end `hideOrbAfterSpeech(600)` (OrbFrame:492). [CODE] — [orch.ts 545, 565, 581](D:\ULTRON\frontend\src\net\orchestrator.ts); [OrbFrame 492](D:\ULTRON\frontend\src\stage\OrbFrame.tsx)
- **Why the Worker-path chains are no-ops.** At `loading(true)` + 600 ms the store is still `thinking` (1 s dwell; the ack's `setState("speaking")` is pending) -> branch (3) returns, chain dead. The chain from the ack callback (~2.1 s after the Ack event) finds `speaking`, and once the ack audio ends nothing moves the state to `idle` (the orchestrator `speak(ack)` has no `onEnd`; unlike the legacy wsBridge ack handler, which returns the state to `thinking` after the ack), so the chain polls silently for ~10 ticks. At the 10-tick limit it calls `finishSpokenResult(currentRequestId ?? "")`; during the ack phase `currentRequestId` is `null` (it is assigned only at result/clarify/error/confirm events and when `orchestrator_process` resolves, which in the log happens AFTER the result), so `null !== ""` makes `finishSpokenResult` return `false` and do nothing. [CODE] — [orch.ts 1113 (assignment after invoke resolves), 371-376 (guard), 451-475](D:\ULTRON\frontend\src\net\orchestrator.ts); [wsBridge.ts 421-441 (legacy ack -> thinking)](D:\ULTRON\frontend\src\net\wsBridge.ts); [log: `process result` printed after `result`, cdp_unified.log ~line 336-340]
- **`stuckSpeakingTicks` is shared by all concurrent chains.** Each Loading(true) and each Ack spawns a new un-cancellable `setTimeout` chain, all incrementing the same module-level counter, so with two live chains the "10 s" limit is reached in roughly 5 s. Combined with the previous point it still does nothing for ack-phase turns, but it will close a turn whose `currentRequestId` is set. [CODE]/[INFER] — [orch.ts 431-433, 462-470]
- **The paths where the orb really is hidden after an ack.** (a) Rust sends the ack as a **Result** via `speak_line` (screen tour: `Ack` then `speak_line(ack_text)`, then `Loading(true)`; slow-notice "Give me a minute, sir." after `SLOW_NOTICE_AFTER_SECS = 6`; CodeRabbit `WORKING_LINES`; reverse-engineer). The frontend `result` handler speaks it and `finishSpokenResult` runs `reset()` -> `idle`; the next tick of the loading chain (state now `idle`) or the idle watchdog then calls `setVisible(false)`. So the orb vanishes about 0.5-2.5 s after the ack finishes while the work (6-20 s) continues. [CODE] — [orch.rs 4755-4775 (tour: Ack, speak_line, Loading true, show_loading), 4870-4882 (slow notice), 8532-8565 (speak_line emits Result)](D:\ULTRON\src-tauri\src\orchestrator.rs); [screen_tour.rs:1084](D:\ULTRON\src-tauri\src\screen_tour.rs); [coderabbit_review.rs:357](D:\ULTRON\src-tauri\src\coderabbit_review.rs); [orch.ts 619-699, 371-396]

## KQ3. When Loading becomes visible, does the orb hide too? Is the spinner placed elsewhere so the user sees neither?

## KQ4. Other mechanisms that can reset or hide the orb mid-ack (idle watchdog, 8 s guard, stuck-speaking watchdog, arbiter drops, `tts-ended`)

### Takeaway
None of the timers fire inside a normal short ack; the realistic ways to lose the orb at or just after the ack are: user hotkey / barge-in, `orb:cancel`, a Result-style ack ending a turn (`reset` + idle watchdog), a dropped/suppressed ack leaving no audio, and stale module state. The exact conditions are below.

### Cited Findings
- **Idle watchdog** (every 500 ms): `isIdleEligible` = `visible && state==='idle' && !ghostActive && !captionActive && !ttsActive && !awaitingInput && !pendingGithubCommand && !loadingVisible && !calibrating`; after `IDLE_HIDE_MS = 2500` of continuous eligibility -> `setVisible(false)`. Busy branch: `state` thinking/listening, `visible`, no loading, no ghost... for `THINKING_STUCK_MS = 60000` (listening 30000) -> `orchestrator_cancel`, `setLoadingVisible(false)`, `reset()`, `setVisible(false)`. ## KQ5. Concrete mechanisms that can make the orb vanish at or just after the ack (ranked)

### Takeaway
Ranked by evidence: (1) Result-style acks (`speak_line`) end the turn on the frontend and the orb idle-hides within ~0.5-2.5 s while Rust work (and possibly the spinner) continues; (2) hotkey/`orb:cancel` barge-in during the ack/result window; (3) store-driven `orchestrator_hide_loading` plus `clear_active_request` leaves no spinner; (4) inert guards (`speechBecameAudible` never reset) mean a stalled or dropped ack is not rescued and the 4 s silent-hide observer never acts; (5) non-native recorder path hides the orb then fails to show the spinner.

### Cited Findings
1. **Result-style ack** — [CODE]: `speak_line` -> `Result` -> `finishSpokenResult` -> `reset()` -> idle -> hide (see KQ2). Applies to screen tour ack, "Give me a minute, sir.", CodeRabbit, reverse-engineer. Source: [orch.rs 4755-4775, 4870-4882, 8532-8565](D:\ULTRON\src-tauri\src\orchestrator.rs), [orch.ts 371-396, 619-699].
2. **User barge-in** — [LOG]: hotkey presses `#7`, `#8` during ack/result; `orb:cancel` -> `visible=false`. Source: [cdp_unified.log ~354-364, 411-428].
3. **Spinner torn down by frontend while Rust still working** — [CODE]: `finishSpokenResult`/`result` `setLoadingVisible(false)` -> `orchestrator_hide_loading`; `signalOrchestratorDone` -> Rust request cleared -> `show_loading` guard rejects re-show. Source: [OrbFrame 655-663], [orch.ts 371-396], [orch.rs 2971-2973, 10002-10005].
4. **Inert recovery guards** — [CODE]: `speechBecameAudible` never reset. Source: [orch.ts 69, 77, 105, 881-883].
5. **Non-native path** — [CODE]: `open_architect` branch hides the orb after a fixed 1200 ms and relies on `setLoadingVisible(true)` that may be rejected as an orphan. Source: [rec 929-935].
6. **Orb stays up in a silent "speaking" pose (the opposite problem)** — [CODE]: after an orchestrator ack finishes nothing returns the state to `thinking`; the legacy ws ack handler did that. Source: [orch.ts 557-585], [wsBridge.ts 435-440].
7. **Duplicate show/hide of the spinner** — [LOG]: two "loading indicator shown" and two "hidden" per turn (Rust + store effect). Source: [nexus_unified.log lines ~7214/7219, 7229/7244, 7359/7364, 7383/7387].

### Inferences
- [INFER] The documented design intent ("orb disappears when the spinner takes over") and the current "never hide unless idle" rule contradict each other for Worker/MCP/GitHub acks, so on those paths the orb persists and the intended hand-off to the spinner never happens; on Result-style paths it happens abruptly and can also remove the spinner.

### Gaps
- No live reproduction (not allowed: read-only, no runtime changes). A reproduction would need a screen-tour or CodeRabbit command and the CDP log, looking for `idle watchdog: hide` or `hideOrbAfterSpeech: speech and caption finished` within 3 s of the ack, followed by only one "loading indicator hidden" before the Result.
