Privacy Policy for NEXUS Assistant

Last updated: October 17, 2026

Pre-release draft. NEXUS is under active development and this policy describes the current development build. It has not been reviewed by a lawyer. It will change as the product does, and the date above will change with it.

1. The short version

NEXUS is local-first, not offline-only. Your memory, settings and file-action history live on your device. To understand speech and answer questions, NEXUS sends data to third-party services, listed in section 3. Nothing is sold, and NEXUS has no advertising or analytics.

2. What stays on your device

  • Wake-word detection. The microphone is monitored on-device for the wake word. Audio is sent anywhere only after the wake word or the hotkey starts a turn.
  • Memory. Facts you told NEXUS, short conversation summaries (30 days), where you left off (7 days), timetable slots you confirmed, and previews of important mail (7 days) are kept in a database encrypted with AES-256-GCM. The key is held in the operating-system credential store. If no credential store is available, the file is kept unencrypted and the app says so. Encryption protects against disk theft and other user accounts, not against malware running as you.
  • Camera (Hand mode). Only when you turn it on. Video frames are processed on-device; only landmark numbers are used and no frames are stored.
  • File actions. Voice file operations are logged locally (an audit log) and deleted files are moved to a .nexus-trash folder, not erased, until you remove them.
  • Settings and API keys. API keys you add are stored in the OS credential store. In this build a legacy plaintext copy may also exist in settings.json; removing it is a planned fix.

Each of these services has its own privacy policy and terms; NEXUS does not control how they handle data once received. The Memory page in the Command Hub keeps a 7-day encrypted log of exactly which memory text each cloud turn sent.

4. Google user data

If you connect a Google account, NEXUS uses Gmail message metadata and snippets (never full message bodies) to alert you to deadlines and important mail, Calendar to read and add events, and Maps services for directions. This data is used only to provide the features you invoke. It is not sold, not used for advertising and not used to train AI models. Google tokens obtained through your own OAuth client are stored in the OS credential store; tokens obtained through the NEXUS Worker are stored in the Worker's database until you disconnect. Subjects, senders or snippets may appear in the memory context pack described above, and sender names may be spoken aloud through Edge text-to-speech.

5. Retention and your controls

  • Memory: conversation summaries 30 days, resume points and mail previews 7 days, facts until you remove them. Say "forget …", use the Memory page, or wipe memory entirely (this also discards the encryption key).
  • Cloud-send log: 7 days, stored encrypted on your device.
  • Worker: OAuth tokens are kept until you disconnect the provider or an administrator removes your device. Daily usage counters are kept per device.
  • Disconnect any account from the Command Hub; remove API keys from the API Keys page.
  • Optional name redaction replaces people's names in memory sent to the cloud with placeholders.

6. Telemetry and logs

NEXUS does not send analytics or crash telemetry. It keeps local usage counters and a local diary on your device. The Cloudflare Worker has request logging enabled; the current code logs message lengths, not message text, but older deployments may have logged fragments of what was said.

7. Children

NEXUS is not directed at children under 13.
